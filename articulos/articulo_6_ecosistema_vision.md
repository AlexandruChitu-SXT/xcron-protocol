Building a protocol isn't just writing code. It's understanding **who** it serves, **why** they need it, and **what** the entire ecosystem gains when it works.

This article answers all three.

## Who Needs XCron?

### 1. The Farmer Who Wants to Sleep in Peace

**Who:** Someone with a position in xExchange, Hatom, or AshSwap farms.

**The problem:** Every day they have to open xPortal, go to the farm, claim rewards, and reinvest. Miss a day, lose money. Go on vacation for a week, lose more.

**What XCron gives them:**
- Automatic daily compound — their money works 24/7
- 18 hours a year they'd spend on repetitive clicks
- Peace of mind knowing nothing gets missed

---

### 2. The Trader Who Needs Discipline

**Who:** Someone who wants to DCA (buy a fixed amount every week) but lacks discipline.

**The problem:** Emotions sabotage their strategy. Some days they buy, others they forget, others they panic and skip.

**What XCron gives them:**
- Automated **DCA** — execution with machine discipline
- No more "should I buy today or wait?"
- On-chain verifiable history that their plan was followed

---

### 3. The DeFi Platform That Doesn't Want to Manage Bots

**Who:** Hatom, xExchange, AshSwap, JewelSwap — any protocol that needs certain functions executed automatically.

**The problem:** Maintaining a centralized bot costs money, requires a senior dev, and if the server goes down for a day, they can lose thousands in unexecuted liquidations.

**What XCron gives them:**
- $0 infrastructure cost — users pay their own deposits
- Decentralized keeper network — if one goes down, 49 others are ready
- Eliminate downtime risk and technical responsibility

---

### 4. The Node Operator Looking for Extra Income

**Who:** One of MultiversX's ~3,200 validators who already has a server running 24/7.

**The problem:** They only earn 8.9% APR from staking. They want to diversify.

**What XCron gives them:**
- Install the Keeper Bot on their existing server — $0 extra cost
- Deposit 1 EGLD as bond — start executing tasks
- Potential net profit that far exceeds staking returns on that same EGLD
- Uses the infrastructure they've already paid for

---

### 5. AI Agents of Tomorrow

**Who:** AI agents operating on-chain using MultiversX's x402, ACP, MCP standards.

**The problem:** An AI agent needs to execute actions at specific times. Without a scheduler, it depends on a human telling it when to act.

**What XCron gives them:**
- Real temporal autonomy — acts on its own when the time comes
- Natively integrated with MultiversX's Agent Economy
- XCron as the "temporal nervous system" of autonomous agents

---

### 6. Any Protocol That Needs Conditional Execution

**Who:** New DEXs, lending protocols, DAOs — anyone on MultiversX who needs "execute this function WHEN price reaches X."

**The problem:** Today, there's no price oracle and no conditional trigger service on MultiversX. If you need automatic liquidations or stop-losses, you have to build and maintain your own bot.

**What XCron gives them:**
- Call `scheduleTask()` with a price condition — done
- XCron keepers monitor the price using a hybrid model (off-chain reads, on-chain execution)
- No bot to build, no server to maintain, no oracle to pay for
- Execution in under 1 second with Supernova

## XCron as Infrastructure

This is the bigger picture. XCron isn't just a product for end users. It's **infrastructure that any protocol on MultiversX can plug into.**

Today, if a protocol needs automated execution, they have two options:
1. Build their own centralized bot (costs money, single point of failure)
2. Do nothing and hope users do it manually

With XCron, there's a third option: **use our decentralized keeper network.**

One smart contract call. One EGLD deposit. Your function executes when the condition is met. No servers, no bots, no maintenance.

This is how we see XCron's role in the ecosystem — the **automation layer** that other protocols build on top of.
