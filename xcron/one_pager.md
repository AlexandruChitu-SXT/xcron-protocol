## The Problem

Smart contracts can't act on their own. Every automated action on MultiversX today — auto-compounding, liquidations, limit orders, DAO execution — depends on centralized servers. This creates single points of failure, censorship risk, and operational complexity.

**MultiversX has no native automation layer. Ethereum has Chainlink Automation. Polygon has Gelato. MultiversX has nothing.**

## The Solution

**XCron** is a decentralized protocol where independent operators (keepers) compete to execute scheduled smart contract functions — triggered by time or on-chain conditions — without centralized infrastructure.

## How It Works

> Developer schedules a task → Deposits EGLD as budget → Keepers monitor 24/7 → Condition met → Keeper executes → Keeper earns reward
